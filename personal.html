03 Influences

People I've learned the most from.

Almost nothing in how I invest is original. It was assembled from people who were generous with their thinking — some of whom I've worked alongside, most of whom I've only read.

Worth keeping this section generous and specific. A list of people you've learned from signals intellectual humility more convincingly than any sentence claiming to have it — and naming a former colleague or mentor alongside the famous names is what makes it read as true rather than curated.
