I'm Brandon Tu. I manage Canadian equity portfolios for institutional clients from Toronto. This site sets out how I think about the market, how I actually work, and what I've built along the way.

01 Orientation

Why this page exists.

Most of what a portfolio manager does is invisible. The output is a number at the end of a quarter; the reasoning that produced it stays in a file somewhere, and the reasoning is the interesting part.

I put this together to make some of that thinking visible. Not because anyone needs convincing — but because writing something down is the most reliable way I know to find out whether I actually understand it. Several of the ideas here got sharper, or quietly fell apart, in the act of being written.

If you've landed here because we're about to talk, this should save us the first twenty minutes.

03 Beyond the Desk

The rest of it.

Judgment isn't built at a desk. Most of what I rely on in markets — patience, comfort with being wrong in public, the habit of separating a bad outcome from a bad decision — I picked up somewhere else entirely.

A few of those places: the hobbies, the volunteer work, and the people whose thinking I've borrowed from most.
